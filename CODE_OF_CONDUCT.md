# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want Mobile to Lappy to be a welcoming and respectful project for everyone.
Anyone participating in project discussions or contributing is expected to
help keep them constructive and inclusive.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Share feedback constructively and focus on ideas, not people.
- Welcome differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Keep communication relevant to the project.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, and
publishing another person's private information without permission are not
acceptable.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to a project maintainer using the contact
details in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports will be reviewed and handled as
confidentially as practical.

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to
protect a respectful project environment.
